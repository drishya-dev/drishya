## Professional native prompt composer

Treat the composer as a core product surface rather than a text-transfer demo. Compact, readable input; clear selected session/harness/workspace and current ownership; properly grouped model/effort and supported policy controls; attachment add/remove/paste/preview; accessible send/stop controls; precise connecting/running/approval/error feedback. Do not simulate unsupported native settings or imply another client's turn is owned by Drishya. Keep permission consent, exact approval identity, redaction and independent observation intact. Review padding, typography, focus, long/multiline drafts, menus, attachments and narrow viewports together. Draft recovery/session isolation and native reconnect acceptance remain explicit.

## Clicks, menus and interaction craft

Audit every interactive control: pressed/focused/selected/disabled/loading/error states, outside-click dismissal, Escape, keyboard entry/return focus, pointer/touch alternatives, menu placement/clipping and clear target sizes. Use informative, interruptible menu/surface arrivals and exits. Do not blindly animate every repeated action: immediate feedback is essential, decorative delay is not. Keyboard actions and reduced-motion/low-resource modes retain instant operation. Apply the motion skill when implementing motion, and the design skill when judging composition and controls. Preserve the builder's single-real-material animations, provenance and independent replay/live capture.

## Recordings Home

Group recordings by the actual session workspace/folder, with meaningful time sections within those groups. Distinguish display names from stable workspace identity; two folders with the same basename must not merge accidentally. Use recorded metadata and local user time boundaries; do not invent roots or dates for missing fields. Provide an explicit unassigned group and honest unavailable timestamps. Make recent/older sessions, search/filtering, selected/native state, counts and collapsing large groups useful at desktop/narrow sizes. Avoid grouping that loses sessions behind sampled counts or confuses session chronology with observed event order.
