## Design direction

Do not compensate for hierarchy with more motion. Keep meaningful origin/arrival/reflow, interruptible reading, immediate repeated controls and reduced-motion paths. Neutral graphite with vivid syntax is coherent. The density problem is uneven: broad empty surroundings, tiny controls, dense clipped code. Focus on readable content and economical chrome before another palette rewrite.

Future critique must operate the real long sessions after these source/frame findings. Preserve the distinction between automated functionality and first-hand sustained experience. No reviewer certified a complete IDE replacement.
