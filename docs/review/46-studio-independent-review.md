# Fresh firsthand reviews: Studio and Classic

11 October 2026 · Three independent reviewers, no inherited conversation

The reviewers explored the working synthetic product in separate tabs before inspecting source. Their briefs covered the whole product, developer adoption and useful missing work; one emphasized design/accessibility and another native-workflow trust. They did not submit native prompts or inspect private recordings. These are critical agent reviews, not human demand validation or proof of real native/platform readiness.

## Verdict

| Reviewer | Studio | Classic | Adoption verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Developer comprehension | 7/10 | 6.5/10 | Evidence companion; strongest path is change → captured patch → later checks |
| Design and usability | 6.5/10 overall, 7 visual | 6/10 overall, 6 visual | Selective review; not yet a primary continuous reader |
| Workflow reliability | 7/10 review workflow; 8 evidence trust | Same core workflow | Occasional review; native readiness unverified |

All three independently preferred Studio's calmer hierarchy while identifying developer utility, rather than animation alone, as the reason to return. Review work and truthful captured/reconstructed/derived/test-order distinctions are strengths. They want review continuity, unresolved-risk collection, assertion-to-source links, curated historical handoff, semantic navigation and real native acceptance before replacing their normal client.

The design reviewer inspected 1280×720, 760×800 and 390×844. Their phone rating was 3/10 Studio / 4/10 Classic; map utility 3/10 both. Those ratings preceded the fixes below. Motion was disabled in their encountered preferences and the browser interface lacked a hover action; their report explicitly does **not** rate hover choreography. Automated browser checks provide separate motion evidence. Do not manufacture improved post-fix ratings.

## Preserved decisions and remaining work

The builder explicitly wants the native/MCP inventory visible, repository relationships and bounded translucent edge chrome. A reviewer finding those visually costly is a tradeoff to assess in human use, not authorization to delete those defining features. Classic stays available. No fake typed reasoning, inferred causal arrows, telemetry, permission weakening or historical/current evidence blending is introduced.

Remaining acceptance: real Claude/Codex account/version/attachment/approval/reconnect/compaction journeys; physical low-resource/platform and multi-agent overlap; fresh long-reading/resource measurements after fuller previews. Remaining product utility: a reversible continuous review trail, unresolved decisions/bookmarks, curated historical evidence packets, supported semantic symbols and precise diagnostic-to-source links. The disabled named-by-test relationship needs a safe resolvable historical location rather than inventing one. Narrow file history still has competing scroll regions and should gain a compact action selector. Utility disclosure accessibility warrants a real assistive-technology pass. These are tracked work, not silently declared complete by the theme change.

Hosted CI remains publishing-only. Local test results and commits belong in STATUS/CONTINUITY once validation finishes. Reviewer scores remain pre-fix evidence.

Local validation: 317 units/typechecks/build and isolated offline packaging pass; 80 regular browser journeys pass across the full run and corrected/new targets. Native real-session and ten-minute opt-ins are skipped. New regressions cover completed-read evidence navigation, preserved Classic live-demo choice, phone viewport clearance and keyboard map labels. A post-fix agent browser recheck was unavailable; no new rating is asserted.
